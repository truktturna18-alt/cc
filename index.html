How it works

1. Choose a denomination. 2. Pay by bank. 3. You get a confirmation email. Bank debits settle in about four business days; your order is queued on confirmation.

Refunds

An unactivated card is refunded in full. Once a card has been activated the balance is held by the card issuer and is not refundable, but a card that fails to activate is replaced or refunded within 24 hours of the order.
